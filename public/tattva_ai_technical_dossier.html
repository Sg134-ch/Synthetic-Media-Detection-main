3. Multi-Modal AI Detection Workflows

A. Image Detection (4-Layer Ensemble)

  • Layer 1 — Facial Localization: OpenCV YuNet ONNX neural face detector crops face bounding boxes with 30% padding for facial artifact models.
  • Layer 2 — Dual Neural Transformers: Parallel inference across OpenAI CLIP ViT-L/14 (zero-shot text-image embedding similarity) and Swin Transformer (texture boundary artifacts).
  • Layer 3 — Error Level Analysis (ELA): Quantifies spatial JPEG compression error matrix against 85% resave baseline to expose non-uniform splicing.
  • Layer 4 — Azimuthal 1D PSD & PRNU: Analyzes 2D-DCT frequency decay against the natural $1/f^2$ law and flags missing physical camera sensor noise (PRNU variance < 0.1).

B. Video Detection & Temporal Anomaly Engine

  • Scene-Aware Sampler: Blends uniform timeline sampling with dynamic frame-difference scene-cut triggers (threshold = 30.0) up to 12 keyframes.
  • Temporal Forensics: Tracks confidence variance, face detection drop rates (momentary glitches), verdict oscillations, and 3D temporal coherence.

C. Audio Detection & Acoustic Forensics

  • Acoustic Transformers: MelodyMachine Wav2Vec 2.0 fine-tuned on commercial voice clone engines (ElevenLabs, Kokoro, Polly) + AASIST graph attention anti-spoofing.
  • Spectral Features: 13-band MFCCs, Spectral Flatness (flags unnaturally smooth TTS energy), Zero-Crossing Rate, and RMS energy consistency.
